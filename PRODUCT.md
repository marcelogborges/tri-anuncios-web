# Tri Anuncios — Product Context

## Product Purpose

Tri Anuncios lets small business owners run paid ads (Meta today, TikTok and Google next) without knowing anything about ads managers. The user describes their business, uploads or generates a creative, picks a package, and the platform handles campaign structure, targeting, budgets, and publishing. It is an MVP built as a TCC (undergraduate thesis) project, evolving toward a real product.

## Users

- Owners of very small businesses in Brazil (local commerce, services): a bakery owner, a nail designer, a small gym.
- Not marketing professionals. They fear tools like Meta Ads Manager; jargon and dense dashboards push them away.
- Mobile-heavy usage. Many will do everything from a phone.
- Low patience for configuration; high need for reassurance that "it's working."

## Register

`product` — the design serves the task. Familiar patterns beat novelty.

## Tone

Encouraging, clear, frictionless. Portuguese (pt-BR) everywhere. Short sentences, zero ad-tech jargon (say "anúncio", not "ad set"). The interface should make an intimidating task feel manageable, never patronizing.

## Strategic Principles

- Every flow must be completable by a first-timer without help.
- Hide platform complexity (campaign/ad set/creative hierarchy) behind simple choices.
- Progressive disclosure: advanced detail only on demand.
- Trust through feedback: always show status honestly (draft, scheduled, processing, live).

## Anti-references

- Meta Ads Manager density and jargon.
- Generic SaaS dashboards stuffed with hero metrics and gradient cards.
- Anything flashy, neon, or gamified; growth here feels steady and organic (see DESIGN.md).
